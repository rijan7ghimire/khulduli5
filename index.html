लामा डकुमेन्ट्रीCinematic documentaries

Cinematic documentaries for YouTube, built with 3D, 2.5D and 2D animation: rebuilt worlds, animated maps and archival material brought to life.

How the documentaries are built
3D

Rebuilt worlds

Places that no longer exist, or that no camera can reach, rebuilt in three dimensions so the story can walk through them.

2.5D

Archive brought to life

Old photographs and paintings cut into layers and given depth, so a still image moves like a shot.

2D

Animated maps

Routes, borders and timelines drawn on screen, so the viewer always knows where the story is.
