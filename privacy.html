Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This page explains what happens to information about you when you visit khuldulinp.com. In short: we collect very little, and we do not track you.

  • No accounts, no payments, no advertising and no analytics.
  • This site sets no cookies of its own.
  • The contact form sends nothing to us. It opens your own email app.
  • Films play through YouTube and TikTok players, which follow their own privacy policies.

01Who we are

khuldulinp.com is the website of खुल्दुली, a Nepali storytelling studio. In this policy, we and us mean खुल्दुली. You can reach us at the email address in section 10.

02When you write to us

The contact form on the home page does not send your message to us or to any server. When you press Send by email, it opens the email app on your device with your message ready. Nothing leaves your device until you send that email yourself.

If you write to us, we receive only what you choose to send: usually your name, your email address, your brand or organisation and your message. We use it only to reply, to prepare a quote and to do any work we then agree on. We keep it for as long as that conversation and that work need, and we delete it when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep it.

The Copy button copies our email address to your device's clipboard. It sends nothing anywhere.

03Choices kept in your browser

When you switch between the dark and light theme, or between English and Nepali, the site remembers your choice in your browser's local storage, on your own device. It is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser's settings.

04Players from other platforms

Our films are shown through embedded players from YouTube and TikTok. A player loads when a film scrolls into view, when you open reel mode, or when you press Full screen. Some poster images also load from YouTube. When this happens, your browser connects to that platform. The platform can receive your IP address and details about your device and browser, and may set cookies or use similar technology under its own privacy policy and terms.

YouTube players on this site use YouTube's privacy enhanced mode. If your device is set to reduce motion, films do not start by themselves, and a player loads only when you choose to open a film.

We do not control what these platforms collect. Their own privacy policies explain it. Links to our pages on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube take you to those platforms, where their own policies apply.

05Hosting and server logs

The site is served by a static hosting provider. Like almost every web server, it keeps standard logs of requests, such as your IP address, the time, the page you asked for and basic details of your browser. The provider keeps these logs to run the service and keep it secure. We do not use them to identify or follow visitors.

Every file of the site, fonts included, is served from the site itself. Apart from the host and the film platforms above, no other service is contacted when you open a page.

06Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. The players described in section 04 may set their own.

07Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only when the law of Nepal requires us to, or, with your agreement, with someone who helps us do work you asked for.

08Your rights

Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018) protects personal information. You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it or delete it. Write to the address in section 10 and we will reply within a reasonable time.

Please do not send sensitive information, such as identity documents or bank details, by email unless we have first agreed a safer way.

09Changes

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
