Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

This page explains what happens to information about you when you visit khuldulinp.com. In short: we collect very little, and nothing that identifies you unless you write to us.

  • No accounts for visitors, no payments and no advertising.
  • Messages sent with the contact form are stored in a database, so that we can reply.
  • We count visits anonymously: no cookies, no IP address, and nothing that follows you to other sites.
  • Films play through YouTube and TikTok players, which follow their own privacy policies.

01Who we are

khuldulinp.com is the website of खुल्दुली, a Nepali storytelling studio. In this policy, we and us mean खुल्दुली. You can reach us at the email address in section 12.

02When you write to us

When you press Send message in the contact form on the home page, the form sends what you typed (your name, your email address, your brand or organisation if you add it, the service you chose and your message) to a database service that stores it for us. Only we can read it. With it go the language the page was in and the random visit number described in section 03, which we use only to stop the form being flooded.

If the form cannot reach that service, it opens the email app on your device with your message ready instead. In that case nothing leaves your device until you send that email yourself.

Whether you write to us through the form or by email, we receive only what you choose to send. We use it only to reply, to prepare a quote and to do any work we then agree on. Section 08 says how long we keep it.

The Copy button copies our email address to your device's clipboard. It sends nothing anywhere.

03Counting visits

To learn which films and pages people use, the site records a few anonymous events: a page being opened, a film being played or opened, reel mode being opened, and a quote button being pressed.

Each event holds only: what happened, the page address, which film or which button, the name of the website that sent you here (only its name, such as facebook.com, never the full address), the language and theme you are using, whether the screen is phone, tablet or desktop size, the time, and a random visit number.

The random visit number is made by your own browser when you open the site and kept in that tab's session storage. It is deleted when you close the tab, it is different on every visit, and it is never linked to your name, your email or your device. It lets us count visits, not only pages.

These events store no IP address and use no cookies and no fingerprinting, and nothing in them follows you to other sites. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, no events are recorded at all.

04Kept in your browser

When you switch between the dark and light theme, or between English and Nepali, the site remembers your choice in your browser's local storage, on your own device. So that the page opens quickly next time, the site also keeps a copy of its own text and film list there. It holds nothing about you. None of this is sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser's settings.

The visit number described in section 03 is kept in session storage and is deleted when you close the tab.

05Players from other platforms

Our films are shown through embedded players from YouTube and TikTok. A player loads when a film scrolls into view, when you open reel mode, or when you press Full screen. Some poster images also load from YouTube. When this happens, your browser connects to that platform. The platform can receive your IP address and details about your device and browser, and may set cookies or use similar technology under its own privacy policy and terms.

YouTube players on this site use YouTube's privacy enhanced mode. If your device is set to reduce motion, films do not start by themselves, and a player loads only when you choose to open a film.

We do not control what these platforms collect. Their own privacy policies explain it. Links to our pages on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube take you to those platforms, where their own policies apply.

06Hosting, database and server logs

The site is served by a static hosting provider. Like almost every web server, it keeps standard logs of requests, such as your IP address, the time, the page you asked for and basic details of your browser. The provider keeps these logs to run the service and keep it secure. We do not use them to identify or follow visitors.

The database service described in sections 02 and 03 also keeps short technical logs of the requests it receives, which can include your IP address. These logs are kept only to run the service and keep it secure. The service's servers may be outside Nepal.

Every file of the site, fonts included, is served from the site itself. Apart from the host, the database service above and the film platforms in section 05, no other service is contacted when you open a page.

07Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no advertising tools or tracking tools from other companies. The visit counting in section 03 uses no cookies. The players described in section 05 may set their own.

08How long we keep it

We chose these periods ourselves:

  • Messages from the contact form: until we have dealt with them, and after that only as long as the conversation or any work that came from it needs. We then delete them, and we delete them sooner if you ask, unless the law requires us to keep them.
  • Visit events: 12 months. After that they are deleted.
  • What is kept in your browser: until you clear it, or for the visit number, until you close the tab.

09Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only when the law of Nepal requires us to, or, with your agreement, with someone who helps us do work you asked for. The database service in section 06 only stores messages for us; it does not use them for its own purposes.

10Your rights

Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018) protects personal information. You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it or delete it. Write to the address in section 12 and we will reply within a reasonable time.

To have a message you sent through the form deleted, write to us from the same email address, or tell us your name and roughly when you sent it. Visit events cannot be linked to anyone, so we cannot tell which of them are yours. They are deleted after 12 months.

Please do not send sensitive information, such as identity documents or bank details, through the form or by email unless we have first agreed a safer way.

11Changes

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
